Privacy Policy

Roi Nueng Rueang Rao Publisher is used to upload and publish promotional videos to the official TikTok account of Roi Nueng Rueang Rao.

Data we access

  • TikTok account identifier and basic profile information.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required for authorized publishing.

How we use data

  • To authenticate the account.
  • To upload and publish videos explicitly selected by the channel owner.
  • To manage publication status and basic publishing responses.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. We do not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.

OAuth tokens are stored locally in a private, access-restricted configuration directory. The account owner may revoke access at any time through TikTok settings.

This application is intended for the channel owner and does not knowingly collect data from children.
